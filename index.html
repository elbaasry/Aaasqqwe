Common Questions

Before You Ask.

How do we get started?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or through the contact form below. We schedule a free discovery call, send you a tailored proposal within 48 hours, and onboarding starts within one week.

Do you work with clients in the UAE?

Yes — AdViral operates in both Egypt and the UAE. UAE clients receive AED quotes, and collaboration runs smoothly remote-first with regular video calls and monthly reporting.

What's included in the monthly packages?

Every package includes content strategy, professional designs, community management, and a monthly performance report. Higher tiers add video production, paid advertising management, and more platforms.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Packages are billed monthly with no long lock-in. We recommend a minimum of 3 months — real brand growth compounds, and that window lets the strategy show measurable results.

Who owns the accounts and the content?

You do — 100%. All accounts, designs, videos, and brand assets belong to your business with full ownership, even if we stop working together.
